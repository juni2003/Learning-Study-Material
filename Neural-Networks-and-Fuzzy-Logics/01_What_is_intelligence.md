# What Is Intelligence in Machines?

## Why This Subject Matters

Modern AI systems power:
- Medical diagnosis
- Recommendation systems
- Fraud detection
- Autonomous vehicles
- Speech & face recognition

Industry does not want memorization.
Industry wants understanding.

---

## What Is an Intelligent System?

An intelligent system:
- Takes input
- Processes it
- Makes decisions
- Improves over time

There are two main approaches:

1. Learning from data → Neural Networks
2. Reasoning with uncertainty → Fuzzy Logic

---

## Key Idea

Humans do not always think in binary (True/False).
We think in degrees.

Example:
Is a patient 100% healthy?
Not always.
Health exists in degrees.

This idea leads us to:
- Neural learning
- Fuzzy reasoning
