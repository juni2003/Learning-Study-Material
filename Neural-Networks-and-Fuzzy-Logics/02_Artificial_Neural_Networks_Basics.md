# Artificial Neural Networks (ANN)

## Biological vs Artificial Neuron

Biological neuron:
- Receives signals
- Processes them
- Fires output

Artificial neuron:
- Receives numbers
- Multiplies by weights
- Adds bias
- Passes through activation function

---

## Basic Structure

Neural networks are made of layers:

1. Input Layer
2. Hidden Layer(s)
3. Output Layer

Each connection has a weight.

Learning = adjusting weights.

---

## Mathematical Form

For one neuron:

z = w1x1 + w2x2 + ... + b  
output = activation(z)

---

## Why Neural Networks Are Powerful

They:
- Learn complex patterns
- Model non-linear relationships
- Work well with large datasets

Used in:
- Image recognition
- NLP
- Speech recognition
- Predictive analytics
